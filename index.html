A free educational toolkit covering essential cybersecurity concepts, safer online habits and ways to protect your accounts.

01 / Password Safety

Learn how to make your accounts harder to compromise.

  • Use long, unique passwords.
  • Use a reputable password manager.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Never share verification codes.

02 / Phishing Awareness

Recognize suspicious messages and misleading websites.

  • Check the sender's address.
  • Be cautious with unexpected links.
  • Watch for urgent or threatening messages.
  • Verify requests through official channels.

03 / Online Privacy

Make thoughtful decisions about your personal information.

  • Review account privacy settings.
  • Limit unnecessary personal information sharing.
  • Keep devices and applications updated.
  • Review app permissions regularly.
